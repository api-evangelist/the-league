The League — 2026 Results in dating are simple to define and hard to manufacture: real relationships, consistently produced, for people who are genuinely trying to find one. Most apps do not measure themselves this way. They measure downloads, daily active users, and time spent in app — metrics that reflect engagement, not outcomes.
