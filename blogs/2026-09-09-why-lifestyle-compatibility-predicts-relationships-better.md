The League — 2026 Chemistry tells you whether a first date will feel good. It doesn’t tell you whether a relationship will work in year three, when the initial spark has settled into daily life — shared calendars, competing priorities, and the thousand small logistics of building something together. That’s what lifestyle compatibility predicts, and it’s a different thing entirely.
